Why we're building SALUS

Health is built one meal at a time. Most apps make that harder than it should be.

Plans for every goal

Pick what you're working towards. SALUS builds the numbers.

Every plan starts from your resting energy, calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which a systematic review found most often within 10% of measured values (Frankenfield 2005). Your activity is added on top, then adjusted for your goal.

Your body report

More than one number.

BMI alone can mislead, especially for muscular people and for South Asians, who face higher health risks at lower BMIs. So SALUS gives a fuller picture.

BMI, with the right cut-offs

Shown with the WHO action points of 23 and 27.5 for Asian populations, alongside the standard 25 and 30 (WHO consultation).

Waist-to-height ratio

Keep your waist under half your height. NICE recommends this simple measure alongside BMI because it reflects belly fat and works across ethnicities (NICE).

Progress over time

A weekly weigh-in trend that smooths out daily water swings, plus optional waist and photo check-ins.

Your real day

Built around how you live.

Two simple questions separate your normal day from your exercise, so nothing is counted twice.

What's your typical day?

Desk job or student · Home and errands · On your feet (teacher, nurse, shop) · Heavy physical work. Based on activity levels set by ICMR-NIN and FAO/WHO (ICMR-NIN).

How do you move on top of that?

Nothing extra · Daily walks · Gym, yoga or sport · Both. Workouts use standard energy values from the 2024 Compendium of Physical Activities.

Already track your burn?

Sync steps and workouts from Google Health Connect or Apple Health, or type in the calories from your watch or gym machine.

Choose your pace

Three ways to follow your plan. Switch any day.

Life changes week to week. Pick how closely you want to track, and change it whenever you like. Every level keeps you inside a safe range.

Flex

For busy weeks, travel or just starting out.

  • Focus on calories and protein only
  • Wider daily range, about ±15%
  • Gentle nudges, a few a day

Steady

For building a lasting habit.

  • Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre
  • Daily range about ±10%
  • Pacing check-ins through the day

Strict

For a competition, a cut or a coach's plan.

  • Every macro and key nutrient tracked
  • Tight range, about ±5%
  • Meal-by-meal targets and detailed reports

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

When is SALUS launching?

Soon, on Android first and then iPhone. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first.

What does the AI Coach do?

It works like a personal trainer and nutrition coach: it learns your goal and routine, gives you one habit at a time, checks in daily, reviews your week and adjusts your calories and workouts. It uses your real SALUS logs, and it never replaces your doctor.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets 7 days of the AI Coach plan free. After that, pick Basic (₹49), Circles (₹79) or AI Coach (₹119) a month, and cancel any time.

Is SALUS a medical app?

No. SALUS is a nutrition and habit tracker, not a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. You can tell SALUS about conditions like diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, high BP, pregnancy or kidney disease (optional), and your plan stays inside safe limits. Always plan your goals with your doctor.

How accurate is photo logging?

AI photo estimates can miss hidden oil and portions, which is why SALUS always lets you adjust portions and oil before saving, and uses its own food database for the final numbers.

Can my coach or dietitian use SALUS with me?

Yes. Enter your coach's plan as a custom plan, or invite them to a Circle where they can see your logging and consistency.

Can my family see my weight?

Never. Circles show streaks, consistency and goal hits only, and you can hide your activity or leave a Circle at any time.

Which foods does SALUS know?

Home-cooked, street, restaurant and packaged food, starting with Indian cuisines and growing worldwide. You can also add your own foods and family recipes.

Who can use SALUS?

Adults 18 and over at launch.
