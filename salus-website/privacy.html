Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: SALUS collects only what it needs to build your nutrition plan and track your meals. We never sell your data, we show no ads, and you can delete your account and all your data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

SALUS ("we", "us") is a nutrition and habit-tracking app run by Dheeraj Nallamotu, India. Contact: salus.team.healthapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and either a password (stored securely and encrypted by our login provider; we never see it) or, if you choose "Continue with Google", your Google name and email.
  • Profile for your plan: first name, sex, age, height, weight, optional waist size, goal, daily activity, exercise, diet preference and tracking level.
  • What you log: foods and portions, water, extra activity, weigh-ins and app settings.
  • Photos and notes you choose to analyse: a meal photo or a short description is sent to our server for analysis. We do not store your meal photos.
  • Health conditions (optional, only with your consent): conditions you choose to tell us about, such as diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, high blood pressure, pregnancy or kidney disease. We use them only to keep your plan inside safe limits.
  • Circles: the Circles you create or join and the display name you use there.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To calculate your calorie and nutrient targets and show your progress.
  • To recognise foods in photos and descriptions.
  • To show your Circle members your consistency score, streak and protein days.
  • To send the reminders you switch on (scheduled on your own phone).
  • To keep the service secure and working.

What others can see

Circle members see only your display name, consistency score, streak and protein days. They never see your weight, body measurements or the foods you eat. The Champions leaderboard is opt-in and shows your first name only.

Service providers we use

  • Supabase stores your account and data on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Google (Gemini API) analyses meal photos and descriptions you send. We use Google's paid API service, under which Google does not use this content to improve its products.
  • Expo delivers app updates.

These providers process data only to run SALUS for you. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, logs, weigh-ins and Circle memberships are deleted from our database straight away. Backups held by our provider are overwritten within their normal backup cycle.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can access, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time. You can edit your profile in the app, and delete everything from Me → Data & privacy → Delete my account and data. For anything else, email salus.team.healthapp@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Our database uses row-level security, so each account can only access its own records. Secret keys for our AI provider are kept on our server, never inside the app.

Children

SALUS is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Health information

SALUS is a nutrition and habit tracker, not a medical device, and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, please plan your goals with a doctor.

Health conditions are optional. We store them only after you tick the consent box, use them only to make your plan safer (for example, no calorie deficit during pregnancy), never show them to your Circles or Champions, and never sell or share them. You can change or delete them any time in Me → Health details, which also withdraws your consent.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
