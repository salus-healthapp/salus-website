Delete your SALUS account

SALUS app by Dheeraj Nallamotu

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open SALUS and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to Data & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email salus.team.healthapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my SALUS account. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and login)
  • Your profile, body details, goal and plan
  • All food logs, water, activity and weigh-ins
  • Your Circle memberships (Circles you created pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only one)
  • Your AI usage counters

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. If you bought a subscription, the payment record is kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies, not by us.
